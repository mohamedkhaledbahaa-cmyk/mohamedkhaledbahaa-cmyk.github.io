Evaluation committees review many bids in little time. A clear, well-ordered proposal earns points before it's even read in detail.

## Core sections

### 1. Cover letter
One page: tender name and number, bid summary and validity.

### 2. Compliance statement
The most important part. A table comparing every spec clause with what you offer:

| Clause | Required | Offered | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.2.1 | 1000 kVA transformer, Dyn11 | 1000 kVA, Dyn11 | Compliant |
| 3.4.5 | IP54 panels | IP55 | Exceeds |

### 3. Datasheets
In BOQ order, with the offered model highlighted.

### 4. Method statement
Execution steps, programme and resources.

### 5. Experience and team
Similar projects and CVs of key engineers.

### 6. Clarifications and deviations
State them clearly in one place. A hidden deviation is worse than a declared one.

> A great technical bid lets the committee find any piece of information in under a minute.
