Cables are among the largest cost items in any electrical project. A small pricing error here affects the whole bid.

## 1. Real quantity vs. BOQ quantity

The BOQ usually reflects drawing lengths. In reality you must add:

- **Wastage** — typically 3–5% depending on drum lengths and cut pieces.
- **Panel entry lengths and bends.**
- **Vertical runs** in multi-storey buildings.

## 2. Material price

Cable prices track copper and aluminium on the commodity markets. When pricing:

1. Get recent quotes from more than one supplier.
2. Note the quote validity period.
3. Add a contingency if delivery is far out.

## 3. Labour and installation

| Factor | Impact |
|---|---|
| Cross-section | Larger cables need more labour and equipment |
| Installation method | Direct burial, conduit, cable trays |
| Height and site access | Adds time and scaffolding |

## 4. Forgotten accessories

- Terminations and joints
- Cable tags
- Ties and cleats
- Insulation (Megger) and HV tests for MV cables

## 5. The formula

```
Item cost = (Qty × (1 + wastage) × unit rate)
          + installation + accessories + testing
          + overheads + margin
```
