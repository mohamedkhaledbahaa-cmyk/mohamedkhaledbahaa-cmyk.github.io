**Every tender teaches something, whether you win or lose.** The teams that improve fastest are the ones that stop after each result and ask, honestly: what worked, and what didn't?

## After every result

- **If we won:** what made the difference — price, technical clarity, programme, relationship? Know it so you can repeat it.
- **If we lost:** price, duration, conditions, scope understanding? Know the gap so you can close it.
- **If there is a negotiation:** review the client's comments and submit a considered revised offer — not a blind discount.

## Three KPIs worth reviewing every quarter

| KPI | What it measures | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| 📈 **Win rate** | Tenders won ÷ tenders submitted | Are we bidding for the right projects? |
| 💲 **Cost deviation** | Estimated vs. actual cost | Is our estimating accurate? |
| ⚠️ **Risk hit rate** | Anticipated risks that actually happened | Are we anticipating the right things? |

## Don't lose the knowledge at handover

Add a **structured handover package** to the execution team:

- pricing and the approved budget,
- assumptions and exclusions,
- supplier and subcontractor offers,
- client correspondence and RFI answers,
- critical contract clauses and the risk plan.

Then the knowledge built during tendering doesn't get lost once execution starts.
