Under EPC contracts the contractor owns the project end to end, usually for a fixed price. Understanding and pricing risk is the difference between profit and loss.

## Key risks

- **Design risk** — errors in load calculations or equipment selection. *Mitigation:* independent design review before procurement.
- **Procurement risk** — late delivery of long-lead items such as transformers and MV switchgear. *Mitigation:* order early and expedite.
- **Price risk** — copper, steel and currency volatility. *Mitigation:* lock supplier prices or negotiate an escalation clause.
- **Site risk** — ground conditions, late site handover, interfaces with other contractors.

## Pricing risk

1. Build a **risk register** with probability and impact.
2. Expected value = **probability × financial impact**.
3. Sum these into a **contingency** line.

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Expected value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transformer delivery delay | 30% | 200,000 | 60,000 |
| Cable price increase | 40% | 150,000 | 60,000 |

## Contract protection

- Check **liquidated damages** and their caps.
- Ensure a **force majeure** clause exists.
- Define **design responsibility** and the documents you rely on.
